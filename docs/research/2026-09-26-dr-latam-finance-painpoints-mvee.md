## 1. What this research contributes

The supplied research suggests that everyday financial conversation centers on making the month work, debt payments, housing affordability, emergency savings, irregular income, and trust in financial products. It describes investing as one interest among these needs, often framed through inflation, bank products, property, or dollar savings.

For the MVEE, the useful hypothesis is **cash-flow fragility: people cannot easily see whether their available money and expected income will cover upcoming obligations, or how today's choices change that situation.**

This does not replace the ecosystem with one feature. It gives its existing surfaces a more concrete question to answer together:

> “¿Cómo llego al próximo ingreso, qué tengo comprometido y qué puedo cambiar?”

The ecosystem still serves general finance questions, market exploration, investments, accounts, budgets, debt, and savings. This research can inform emphasis, examples, and connected journeys without making every visitor follow the same financial ladder.

An app can reduce uncertainty, missed obligations, recording effort, and opaque costs. It cannot create income or guarantee that a household can afford its essential expenses. A negative forecast must not be treated as a failure of discipline.

## 2. Evidence limits

This attachment is an agent's narrative synthesis, not a documented social-media survey. It supplies creator names and institutional references but no post URLs, dates of collection, sample size, selection method, comment extracts, or linked statistical reports.

Consequently:

- Creator topic descriptions are leads to examine, not verified current posting patterns.
- Words such as “dominant,” “most important,” and the numbered pain ranking express the source author's assessment; they do not establish prevalence.
- Influencer supply and engagement can reflect marketing funnels, algorithms, and commercial incentives. They do not directly measure unmet need, willingness to share data, product use, or retention.
- Claimed statistics about wages, household baskets, credit balances, delinquency, account ownership, and consumer worries need original reports, dates, populations, and definitions before publication or decision-critical use.
- Comparing an individual formal wage with a household consumption basket is not automatically a like-for-like affordability measure.
- Country characterizations must not become assumptions about every Dominican or Latin American user. Evidence from another country does not establish DR demand.
- Claims about regulation, bureau rules, data rights, bank coverage, product capabilities, and lending eligibility require current primary-source verification.

No independent source verification was performed for this intake. The date above is when Argus recorded the material, not evidence that every statement describes September 2026.

## 4. How each surface can become more relevant

These are proposed refinements within the approved structure. They do not silently add all research suggestions to committed scope.

### Home

Emphasize the near-term financial picture alongside what the person has and owes. A short list of upcoming income and commitments may answer the user's immediate question better than another chart. A calendar or timeline is a possible presentation, not a newly locked design.

Use phrases such as “Estimated remaining until your next recorded income” rather than an unconditional “safe to spend.” Show the period, included obligations, freshness, and missing information. If the records are insufficient, offer to add the missing facts rather than calculate a reassuring fiction. Keep currencies separate.

### Accounts

Make cash, informal debts, installment obligations, and irregular income easy to describe without judgment. Capture card limits and billing facts when known, without blocking basic recording when they are unknown.

Distinguish actual receipts, scheduled obligations, and forecast activity. Monthly, twice-monthly, and every-two-weeks schedules are not interchangeable. An expected remittance is not received money; a transfer from an owned account is not new income.

### Argus

Use everyday language in optional starters: understanding a minimum payment, a changed bill, an offer, or the next quincena. Preserve market questions and existing calculators. Let people move from explanation to a reviewable entry or saved plan without forcing that transition.

Offer explanation without shame. Do not adopt the source's “make the card the villain,” “apariencia,” or “investing is theater” framing as default product language. Expense categories belong to the user's understanding of their life.

### Plan

Make budgets useful for someone with little or no surplus, not only someone allocating excess income. Support revising a plan when an expected payment does not arrive or an emergency occurs. Goals should remain user-selected.

Fixed, flexible, and occasional expenses are a candidate simple grouping to test. They should not be imposed as verified local preference or replace the ability to inspect actual activity.

Debt comparisons can explain how different payment approaches behave under explicit assumptions. Housing affordability is a potential application of calculation and planning, not a promise to qualify someone for a mortgage.

### Search

Make retrieval practical: find a past charge, uploaded statement, due date, rate explanation, or comparison. Future discovery should surface maintained sources and commercial disclosures, not turn content popularity into product endorsement.

### Updates

Favor useful timing: an approaching recorded payment, a goal milestone, a changed assumption, or stale account information. Let users choose reminders and summaries. Distinguish a schedule-based reminder from an event observed in a bank feed.

Keep financial amounts inside the authenticated app, consistent with the MVEE. The source's amount-bearing notification examples are not approved push/email copy. Do not instruct users how to spend a remittance based only on its anticipated arrival.

### Profile and controls

Make data source, permissions, notification settings, correction, deletion, and export understandable. Do not interpret “household” as permission to expose a partner's records. Household collaboration has subsequently been approved, with explicit membership, consent, and visibility boundaries owned by MVEE section 12.

## 5. Ingestion implications

The MVEE's common capture → review → confirm → record flow remains the foundation. This research adds useful contexts to test against it:

| Entry method | Relevant examples | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manual | Cash on hand, a colmado debt, a school payment, a received commission | Opening balance versus activity; own account versus external income; amount owed versus payment made |
| Typed chat | “Me entraron RD$8,000 de comisión”; “El viernes creo que me pagan” | The first can propose received income; the second proposes an expectation, not an actual receipt |
| Voice | Several small cash purchases or an informal repayment | Correct names, amount, currency, payer/payee, and direction before saving |
| File/statement | Card charges, loan payment schedule, bank statement | Billing period, statement balance versus new transactions, fees, duplicates and overlap |
| Camera/screenshot | Receipt, bank offer, WhatsApp payment confirmation | A sent message or screenshot may not prove settlement; a supplied offer is not a financial transaction |
| Device-assisted payment capture | A payment-related event near the time of purchase | Detected event versus confirmed settled purchase; match against later imports |
| Bank/API/browser-assisted access | Fresh balance or posted transactions where permitted and supported | Freshness, pending versus posted, coverage, duplicates, consent and access failures |

User-selected WhatsApp screenshots fit the existing image-input direction. They do not authorize reading WhatsApp conversations or building a messaging integration. SMS/email parsing appears in the research as a future possibility; it is not added to the locked MVEE by this intake and would need explicit consent, access design, and coverage validation.

Document intake has two different outcomes: **record financial facts** or **explain a document**. A bank offer, credit report, or mortgage advertisement must not automatically create an account, debt, or transaction. Confirmation is necessary but does not repair missing or misleading source data by itself.

Income uncertainty also matters to freshness. Capture whether an amount is received, expected, estimated, or overdue; expose assumptions in forecasts instead of presenting optimistic expectations as bank balances.

## 6. Connected journeys suggested by the research

### Can I cover the next quincena?

Record received cash and bank balances → add known bills and expected income → inspect the dated estimate on Home → ask Argus what is included or missing → adjust a budget → update the record when money actually arrives.

### Why is this card balance not falling?

Upload a statement → confirm extracted purchases, fees, interest, and payments → inspect the account → ask for a payoff scenario → save a chosen plan → record subsequent activity → explain changes in actual balance and projected payoff separately.

### Can this housing payment fit my life?

Supply an offer and personal assumptions → explain rate, term, known fees, and missing conditions → compare payment scenarios against recorded obligations and variable income → optionally save a housing goal. Argus does not declare approval or guarantee an institution's decision.

### Where did the emergency buffer go?

Record an unexpected expense and its funding source → reflect the actual account and goal change → revise the plan without blame → preserve useful history rather than treating the goal as failed.

These journeys exercise several surfaces and input methods. They preserve the ecosystem approach rather than reducing the product to one acquisition hook.

Preserve the MVEE's structure. Use this research to make it more concrete around near-term commitments, irregular income, cash, debt costs, and understandable financial choices.

## 10. Niche-centered ecosystem interpretation

**Subsequent decision:** The founder approved this audience and experience emphasis on September 26, 2026. The canonical locked wording now lives in [MVEE section 1.1](../specs/argus-minimum-viable-ecosystem-experience.md#11-locked-audience-and-experience-emphasis), with pain-point coverage and the reinforcing loop in section 11. The proposal below is retained as decision history; other research recommendations were not blanket-approved.

The founder's clarification changes the emphasis of the interpretation: narrow the people and circumstances the ecosystem is designed around, without reducing the ecosystem to one isolated feature.

### Proposed initial group

Dominican residents managing money across cash, at least one bank account, and a credit card or installment debt; income arrives by quincena or varies; they already try to keep track through notes, bank apps, spreadsheets, or memory but cannot confidently see what remains after commitments.

This is a proposed customer focus, not a newly locked eligibility rule. Users do not need all these attributes to benefit. Neither income level nor financial knowledge alone defines the group. The shared situation is fragmented information plus a recurring decision about available money.

### Proposed experience promise

“Know what is already committed, what remains until the next income, and what changes when you make a decision.”

The ecosystem should help answer three recurring questions:

1. Where do I stand right now, based on the information I have recorded?
2. Can the upcoming obligations fit before the next income, and what assumptions affect that answer?
3. How does a purchase, payment, delay in income, or goal contribution change the picture?

### Practical surface emphasis

| Surface | Niche-specific emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Near-term obligations, received versus expected income, and estimated remaining money; asset/debt summaries remain available |
| Accounts | One place to reconcile cash, bank balances, cards, installments, and informal debt, with visible freshness |
| Argus | Turn messy descriptions/documents into reviewable entries; explain costs; explore “what changes if…” using recorded facts |
| Plan | Connect spending commitments, debt payments, and savings goals to the same available money; avoid budgeting the same money twice |
| Search | Recover the transaction, agreement, statement, or previous answer that explains a number |
| Updates | Prompt an appropriate action when a recorded deadline, threshold, or relevant fact changes; avoid generic engagement prompts |

An available-to-spend estimate and an account balance are different facts. The estimate must identify the period and reserved obligations, separate currencies, and expose uncertainty. Planned payments are commitments until paid; when actual activity arrives, match it against the commitment rather than subtracting both.

### Proposed signature journey

Someone receives income and records it by voice. Argus proposes the entry and the person confirms. Accounts records the receipt; Home shows the money alongside upcoming commitments; Plan reflects the relevant allocations. Later the person uploads a card statement and reviews the matches, rather than re-entering or double-counting purchases. A hypothetical extra payment can be explored in chat before any actual financial record changes.

The value is cumulative: each confirmed piece of information makes the person's next question easier to answer. Returning should take less effort than rebuilding the picture from separate bank screens and memory.

### How existing Argus contributes

Tracking supplies personal context. Existing grounded calculations, explanations, comparisons, and historical research help the person understand consequences. Preserve those capabilities as accessible tools; do not gate market questions behind debt repayment or require a particular life stage.

Housing and investing can be aspirations supported by the same record and scenario tools. They should not distract the first-use experience from the immediate question that brought the person in, nor require extra top-level destinations.

### Feedback that should shape the ecosystem

When people use it, learn which uncertainty it removed, what they still calculated elsewhere, which input was too burdensome, and whether the information was current enough to trust. Repeatedly reaching for the product around real decisions is more meaningful than enthusiastic reactions to the demo.
