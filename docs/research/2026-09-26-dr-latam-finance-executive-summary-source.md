## Playing field — one page

Three separate markets are being talked about as if they were one. They are not.

1. **Mass personal finance** (budget, debt, house, inflation). This is where almost all attention and pain sit.
2. **Basic investing** (“don’t leave cash in the account”). Growing, still shallow.
3. **Serious trading / backtesting.** Almost empty in the public conversation. That is a niche on top of (1) and (2), not a substitute for them.

## Who owns the microphone

**DR, actual finance creators (not politicians):** Jahaira Roble (mortgages), Juan Carlos Guilbe (debt, cards, “aparentar”), Aura Cruz (budgets, debt snowball, scams), plus Banco Popular’s AI persona GIA. Distribution is Instagram/TikTok/YouTube, conversion is WhatsApp groups and paid courses.

**LatAm template they copy:** Colombia’s Mis Propias Finanzas (invest by time horizon, inflation, MoneyCon), plus Mexico (Moris Dieck, Nu-style neobanks), Argentina/Chile coaches. Same funnel: Reel → free group → masterclass.

**What they post:** casa/hipoteca, tarjetas, apariencia vs patrimonio, “ahorrar ya no alcanza,” emergencies, named local scams. Not strategies, fees, or honest performance.

X is secondary. Banks and coaches monetize literacy that still ends in *their* product.

## What users actually hurt from

Ranked by evidence, not by influencer talking points:

1. **The month does not close.** Wages lag the basket. Growth is macro; cash is micro.
2. **Inflation + job instability + housing cost** are the top stated fears (TransUnion: inflation ~57%, jobs ~47%, housing ~50%).
3. **The house is the dream and the trap.** People reserve apartments, then fail underwriting. Cuotas eat the budget because variable income and other debts were ignored.
4. **The card is a second paycheck.** A large share of balances revolve; new volume is concentrated in low-income and informal workers; those same segments have the worst mora. Minimum payment is the survival tactic.
5. **No shock absorber.** One medical bill or broken car becomes new debt or a loan from family.
6. **Credit is wanted and rationed.** Almost everyone says access matters; about a third say they have enough.
7. **Status spending is real** — and so is paying for *comida* on revolving credit. Do not moralize only the Flex.
8. **Informal / irregular income** breaks bank math. Households run on salary + comisión + remesa.
9. **Scams and distrust.** Inclusion rose (accounts ~65% of adults), trust in yield products did not.
10. **“I should invest” with no surplus.** That gap is how bad trading signals get a hearing.

Education is not the core wound. **Liquidity and opaque credit** are.

## What rich-country apps already solved

They did not abolish thin margins. They made the next 30 days and the true cost of credit visible.

| Job | How US/EU apps do it | Transfer to DR/LatAm? |
|---|---|---|
| See the month | Plaid aggregation, “safe to spend,” bills calendar | **Job yes. Pipe no.** DR has no open-banking mandate. |
| Stop leaks | Find/cancel subscriptions, negotiate bills | **Pattern yes.** Local leaks are planes, cuotas, cable — not Netflix. You will not have Rocket Money’s call center. |
| Change behavior | YNAB: every peso gets a job | **Yes. Best portable idea.** Works without bank APIs. |
| Price credit | Score + factors + utilization | **Yes, as an explanation layer.** ProUsuario + TransUnion + Data-Crédito already exist; nobody unifies them well. |
| Housing / goals | DTI, payoff date, projections | **Yes, and more urgent here** than a net-worth graph. |
| Invest / net worth | Fee analysis, allocation | **Only after revolving is visible.** Otherwise it is theater. |

Nubank / Ualá / Mercado Pago already shipped the *bank-inside-the-app* version of this in BR/MX/CO/AR. DR is behind on rails and still bank-app fragmented (Popular, Banreservas, BHD, Qik). A third-party PFM cannot assume it will sync those accounts on day one.

## What a DR/LatAm app should steal — and what it should not

**Steal**
- Quincena / remesa / comisión as income types, not “salary.”
- DOP + USD on one calendar.
- Three buckets: fijo, flexible, no-mensual.
- Envelopes + manual entry + photo/PDF of statements (fake Plaid).
- Card mínimo → extra interest and months added, in pesos.
- Informal *prestamista* in the same debt list as the bank.
- Hipoteca simulator: *apartar ≠ calificar*; residual after real life, not after the brochure cuota.
- Guided bureau pull + “why they said no.”
- Couple/household sharing via WhatsApp.
- Goal ladder: mínimos → fondo de paz → kill revolving → casa or USD buffer → then invest.

**Do not steal**
- Aggregation as the core loop.
- Custody / high-yield sweep (that is a bank).
- Fake richness via home-value net worth.
- Investment social feed.
- Credit-builder / payday-adjacent advances. The market already has that product. It is the card mínima.

## Strategic read

The public fight in DR and LatAm is **cash-flow survival and a house**. Coaches, banks, and neobanks are all selling a version of “order your money.” None of them sell honest strategy performance.

That leaves a clean split:

- If you want **distribution**, speak mortgage, card mínimo, and “will this quincena close.” That is the language already trained.
- If you want **differentiation** (the Argus-shaped hole), gate trading/backtesting behind “you can see your revolving cost.” Do not enter as another finfluencer yield story. Trust is scarce and regulation of paid promotions is tightening.

Bottom line: the field is crowded at literacy-and-coaching, empty at truthful tools, and technically blocked on US-style bank sync. Win the month-in-pesos first. Everything else is a later module.
