**Status:** draft for review. Docs only. No migration, code or env change.
**Base:** branched from `codex/private-alpha-next` at `8c3869f06bd8` ("docs(env): correct reader evidence for seven retained-control rows (#1543)"), read on October 30, 2026. Every file:line below is at that commit.

## Summary

The three biggest gaps between the current and the target model:

1. **Receipt facts live in JSON, so the database cannot enforce them.** RNC, NCF, amount, date and kind sit inside `financial_import_events.resolution`. Only sales have a database-enforced NCF-once rule (`supabase/migrations/20261021050000_business_sale_ncf_once.sql:42`). Purchases have no RNC plus NCF unique key, and duplicates are decided in code on merchant, date, amount and currency (`src/argus/domain/business/duplicates.py:25`).
2. **Delete behavior contradicts money retention.** 37 foreign keys to `spaces` are `on delete cascade`, and import events cascade from `auth.users` (`supabase/migrations/20261002120300_financial_import_reconciliation.sql:12`). Deleting a Business or a person can remove receipts, entries and history. The 606 and 607 exports leave no durable record of what was sent.
3. **Business shares one schema with 64 tables it does not use**, and kept tables still point at them. 23 old Argus, 3 guest-only, 9 leftover, 27 Personal or household and 2 chat-telemetry tables with no clear owner sit beside the 99 Business and shared tables and 3 shared tables kept outside Business (the marketing waitlist, the compute-ceiling counters and in-app feedback). `backtest_runs` has 7 inbound foreign keys, including from kept tables, and guest columns and policies remain on `profiles`, `conversations`, `messages` and `usage_counters`. There is no per-receipt processing trail.

Decision labels. D7 to D13 are the needs-Lucas items of the removal plan steps with the same ids (`/workspace/specs/db-removal-plan-2026-10-30.md`, not in the repo). No separate D7 to D13 decision definitions exist in `docs/`. The wording of each decision in section 5 is **inferred** from those plan steps.

## 1. Current model

166 persistent tables (167 created, `business_email_ai_consents` dropped at `supabase/migrations/20261014120000_business_ai_consents.sql:93`). This matches the 166 in #1431. Each row was reconciled against the same-day repo-only schema map (`/workspace/specs/schema-map-repo-only-2026-10-30.md` and its `.refs.csv`, not in the repo) and re-checked on this branch. "Owner in schema map" is that map's label; the group heading is this draft's verified class. FKs come from the creating and altering migrations. Reader and writer cells give the first app-code reference (src, scripts, web; tests excluded) that runs SQL against the table, then `(+n)` more. Delete sweeps and table lists (`custody_graph.py`, account deletion) are skipped when a real query exists. Where no direct SQL exists, the cell says which case applies: **truly none in app code** (tests only, or no reference at all), **SQL function** (written only inside a migration function, called by RPC), **name constant or dynamic SQL** (the table name sits in a constant or table map that the scan does not follow; the real query line is given where found), or **library-managed**.

Classification rules:

- **Business** means `spaces`, every `business_*`, `whatsapp_*` and `dgii_*` table.
- **Shared (Business uses)** means auth-adjacent and platform tables plus the `financial_*` intake, record and activity tables that Business rows also use, scoped by `owner_space_id`. Which of `conversations`, `messages` and `chat_turn_lifecycles` Business needs is not proven from the repo. D1's staging inventory confirms it.
- **Leftover** means private alpha, early access, beta invites and checkpoint bookkeeping.
- **Guest only** and **old Argus** follow the removal plan.
- **Personal/household** means the consumer app's household and planning tables. They are outside the Business target and outside the removal plan. They stay untouched here.

### Findings that correct the removal plan

From a repo-only schema map made the same day, re-checked on this branch:

- **`route_receipts` is written only by the chat path.** The only insert is `src/argus/domain/supabase_gateway.py:1668`, reached from `src/argus/api/routers/agent.py:672`. Document extraction only puts receipts in its result metadata (`src/argus/domain/ingestion/documents/extractor.py:613`). The plan's D11 reason for keeping it ("document extraction writes it") does not hold at the tip.
- **Old Argus tables missing from the plan's drop steps:** `public_excerpt_snapshots`, `refusal_observations`, `argus_memory_vectors` and the nine `memory_*` tables.
- **`cost_ledger_entries` has no clear owner** and references `route_receipts` (`supabase/migrations/20260702000001_add_cost_ledger_entries.sql:29`, `on delete set null`). Dropping `route_receipts` needs that column detached first, as D7 does for backtest links.
- **Four consent tables overlap:** `business_ai_consents`, `business_receipt_reading_consents`, `business_whatsapp_ai_consents` and `business_consent_acceptances`. Section 2 collapses them into one.

## 2. Target model (Cuadrao Business only)

Rules for every table below:

- **Scope.** Every row carries `space_id` (the Business). Every cross-table reference is a composite foreign key on `(id, space_id)`, so a row cannot point at another Business. Today this holds for `business_sources` (`supabase/migrations/20261010223000_business_contract_foundation.sql:70`) and must hold everywhere.
- **RLS stance.** RLS on, `public`, `anon` and `authenticated` revoked, `service_role` only. The API is the only path. The browser never reads tables directly. `business_consent_acceptances` grants select to `authenticated` today (`supabase/migrations/20261019050000_business_consent_acceptances.sql:23`). The target removes that grant and serves it through the API.
- **Delete behavior.** Nothing that holds money or its evidence cascades from `spaces` or `auth.users`. Those foreign keys become `on delete restrict`. Removal happens only through the closed-Business purge path, under the retention rule #1446 decides. Operational rows (leases, outboxes, read states) may cascade.
- **Never holds, everywhere:** raw secrets or tokens, full card or bank account numbers, raw phone numbers or email addresses where a hash works, OCR full text, and data from another Business.

### Guest removal safety

From the October 30 guest-removal security triage (`/workspace/scan-triage/guest-removal-security-2026-10-30.md`, tip `8c3869f`). File and line references re-checked on this branch.

- **R1 (High). Keep a permanent anonymous refusal in `current_user`.** Anonymous tokens take the guest branch at `src/argus/api/dependencies.py:561-591`. If that branch is deleted, an anonymous token falls through to `permanent_account_access_allowed` (`src/argus/api/guest_access.py:48-54`). With public account access on in production, that check allows an empty email, and the request gets a registered context. The guest branch is replaced, not deleted. It refuses `is_anonymous` with a 401, permanently. `permanent_account_access_allowed` also refuses an empty or blank email: today with public access on it returns `not gateway.private_alpha_email_disabled(email)` (`src/argus/api/guest_access.py:52-53`), and `private_alpha_email_disabled` has no row for an empty email (`src/argus/domain/supabase_guest_accounts.py:43-50`). Source: `/workspace/scan-triage/guest-removal-security-2026-10-30.md` R1. The `is_anonymous` predicates in Business, recording, household and transfer SQL stay as the backstop.
- **R2 (Medium). Do not delete the compute ceiling file blindly.** `check_guest_compute_ceiling` is the only path to the registered per-user daily ceiling (`src/argus/api/chat/guest_compute_ceiling.py:117-126`), called from `src/argus/api/routers/agent.py:416` and `:419`. Section 4 steps 13 and 15 follow this order. Both are one-way and go to Lucas through Yelena.
